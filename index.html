01How much protein is in each laddu?

8 to 10g depending on the flavour. Peanut Gur Caramel has the most at 10g. Each laddu is about 50g.

02Is there added sugar?

No refined sugar. We use jaggery and dates, which keeps each laddu at 3 to 4g of sugar.

03How long do they keep?

Best within 45 days. Keep the can closed somewhere cool and dry. No fridge needed, though the chocolate one is good cold.

04Vegetarian? Gluten-free?

Every flavour is vegetarian. They contain milk, nuts and oats, and are made in a kitchen that also handles gluten.

05Where do you ship?

Anywhere in India. Orders leave within 24 hours and take 2 to 5 days. Free shipping over ₹999.
